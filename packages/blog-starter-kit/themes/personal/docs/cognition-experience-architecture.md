# Cognition Experience Architecture

Abstract Algorithms should feel like a guided engineering evolution, not a feature catalog or long-form blog archive.

## Homepage Journey

Flow:

1. Identity: name the platform promise and invite the learner into a role.
2. What you want to become: select Backend Engineer, AI Engineer, Distributed Systems, Staff Architect, or Interview Prep.
3. AI-guided entry: ask the mentor from the current mental state.
4. Continue learning: resume exact progress or start the recommended article.
5. Interactive systems exploration: navigate concept relationships through the knowledge graph.
6. Practice and pressure-testing: launch simulations that break assumptions.
7. Interview readiness: translate understanding into tradeoff explanations.
8. Continue your journey: one persistent path across articles, graph, mentor, and practice.

CTA rule:

- One dominant CTA per section.
- Secondary actions are text links or expandable tray actions.

## Article Cognition Interface

The article route keeps Hashnode MDX, SEO, GraphQL fetching, and editorial content. The visible structure is now a cognition interface:

1. Executive TLDR
2. Living topology
3. Mental model
4. Production tradeoffs
5. Failure pressure-testing
6. Interactive simulation
7. Interview reasoning
8. Deep technical expansion
9. Related systems graph
10. Continue learning

The MDX article is preserved as an expandable deep technical reference. It remains rendered by the existing `MarkdownToHtml` pipeline.

## Mobile Cognition Feed

Mobile should not compress desktop complexity. It follows a progressive cognition loop:

Concept -> Visual -> Tradeoff -> Challenge -> Continue

Mobile interaction model:

- Bottom tray exposes one primary next action.
- Secondary actions live inside the tray.
- Topology nodes are horizontally swipeable.
- Deep reference content is expandable.
- AI helper remains available from the tray and embedded mentor blocks.

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve canonical article routes.
- Preserve article metadata, OG, JSON-LD, and MDX rendering.
- Preserve Hashnode GraphQL content APIs.
- Add cognition layers locally over the article-first architecture.
